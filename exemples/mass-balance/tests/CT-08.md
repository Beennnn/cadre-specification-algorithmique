# CT-08 — Target mass not a multiple of the step

| | |
|---|---|
| **Case** | `CT-08` |
| **Specification** | [SPEC-MAS-001 §10](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#ct-08--target-mass-not-a-multiple-of-the-step) v1.1.0 |
| **Reference data** | [`reference-data.csv`](../code/src/test/resources/reference-data.csv), line `CT-08` |
| **Discriminating** | **Yes** — it is the only case where the *batch*, not the recipe, is impossible |

## What it covers

| Item | |
|---|---|
| [`E-MAS-004`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#e-mas-004) | `target_batch_mass` is not a multiple of `balance_step` → reject |

## The data

`target_batch_mass = 1.000 kg`, **`balance_step = 0.300 kg`**

| Component | Fraction |
|---|---|
| `CMP-A` | 0.500000 |
| `CMP-B` | 0.500000 |

1.000 is not a whole number of 0.300 steps — it is 3⅓. **Expected: rejected with
`E-MAS-004`.**

## Why the case exists

Because `INV-01` demands **exact** conservation, and every weighed dose must be a whole
number of steps ([`INV-02`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#inv-02)). A sum of whole steps is a
whole number of steps. If the target is not one, the two invariants cannot both hold —
whatever the recipe, whatever the rounding, whatever the allocation.

**The batch is arithmetically impossible, and nothing about the components can fix it.**
That is why the check looks only at the target mass and the step, before the components are
even examined.

Left unchecked, the failure is not loud. Two doses of 0.500 kg round to 2 steps and 2 steps
— 0.600 kg each, 1.200 kg total, a residual of −0.200 kg, which is not a whole step either.
The allocation then produces a dose of 0.400 kg, and `INV-02` finally fails, deep in the
calculation, with a message about a component rather than about the request.

> This is what a precondition buys: the same defect is caught **at the door, named
> `E-MAS-004`**, instead of surfacing three rules later as an invariant violation nobody
> can act on.

## What it does not test

| | |
|---|---|
| Near-misses | 1.000 against a step of 0.300 is off by a third of a step. A target off by a millionth would be rejected too, and no case checks that the comparison is exact rather than tolerant |
| Any nominal behaviour | The calculation is never entered |
