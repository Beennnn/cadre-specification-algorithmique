# CT-10 — Nominal masses exactly on a half-step

| | |
|---|---|
| **Case** | `CT-10` |
| **Specification** | [SPEC-MAS-001 §10](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#ct-10--nominal-masses-exactly-on-a-half-step) v1.1.0 |
| **Reference data** | [`reference-data.csv`](../code/src/test/resources/reference-data.csv), line `CT-10` |
| **Discriminating** | **Yes** — it is the only case that decides `P-01` |

## What it covers

| Item | |
|---|---|
| [`P-01`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#p-01) | Rounding mode for doses — `HALF_EVEN` |
| [`RG-020`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#rg-020--rounding-to-the-balance-step) | Rounding to the balance step, **at a tie** |
| [`RG-050`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#rg-050--allocation-of-the-residual) | Allocation of the residual |

## The data

`target_batch_mass = 1.000 kg`, `balance_step = 0.001 kg`

| Component | Fraction | Nominal | In steps | Rounded | **Weighed** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CMP-A` | 0.250500 | 0.250500 | **250.5** | 0.250 | **0.250** |
| `CMP-B` | 0.249000 | 0.249000 | 249.0 | 0.249 | **0.249** |
| `CMP-C` | 0.500500 | 0.500500 | **500.5** | 0.500 | **0.501** |

Two nominal masses land exactly halfway between two steps. `HALF_EVEN` sends both to the
even neighbour — 250 and 500. Sum of rounded `= 0.999` → `residual = +0.001` → `CMP-C`,
the largest fraction. **Final sum = 1.000 kg.**

## Why the case exists

Because until v1.1.0, `P-01` was decided by nothing.

`HALF_EVEN` was chosen deliberately, and the specification explains why: rounding "half up"
pushes masses upwards on every tie, and across a production run that drift is a real cost.
The parameter has an owner, a value and an effective date. It is implemented as a named
constant. It was listed as covered.

And in nine cases, **not one nominal mass ever fell on a tie**. 0.333333 kg is 333.333
steps; 0.142857 kg is 1.42857 steps; 0.140 kg is 1.4 steps. Every rounding in the set was
unambiguous. Swap `HALF_EVEN` for `HALF_UP` in the implementation and all nine cases still
passed.

Here the two modes part company:

| | `CMP-A` | `CMP-B` | `CMP-C` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HALF_EVEN` — the specified one | **0.250** | 0.249 | **0.501** |
| `HALF_UP` | 0.251 | 0.249 | 0.500 |

> **The invariants do not catch this.** Both sheets sum to exactly 1.000 kg, both use whole
> steps, neither has a negative dose. `INV-01` to `INV-04` all hold under the wrong rounding
> mode. What catches it is the **approved masses** — the business said 0.250 and 0.501, and
> only those. This is the clearest illustration in the repository of why a reference set is
> not the same thing as a set of properties.

## How the harness knows

The test does not take the claim on trust. For each case it recomputes every nominal mass
in steps and checks whether any lands on an exact half — the one place where the two modes
disagree — and reports it:

```
| `CT-10` | OK | 3 doses · residual 0.001 kg (1 step(s)) · INV-01 to INV-03 hold
                 · **decides `P-01`** — a nominal mass falls on a half-step |
```

The [test report](../reports/TEST-REPORT.md) ends on a table of the parameters and whether
**any** case decides them. A parameter nothing arbitrates prints as *its value is never
arbitrated*.

## What it does not test

| | |
|---|---|
| A tie that rounds *up* | 250.5 and 500.5 both round **down** to their even neighbour. A nominal mass at 251.5 steps would round up to 252, and no case covers that direction |
| The residual bound | One step against `P-02` = 3 |
| The tie-break of `RG-050` | `CMP-C` is the strict largest fraction |
