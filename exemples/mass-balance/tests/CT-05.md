## What it covers

| Item | |
|---|---|
| [`RG-040`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#rg-040--acceptability-of-the-residual) | Acceptability of the residual, **at the bound** |
| [`P-02`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#p-02) | Maximum tolerated residual, in balance steps |
| [`RG-020`](../spec/SPEC-MAS-001.en.md#rg-020--rounding-to-the-balance-step) | Rounding, on a **coarse** step |

## The data

`target_batch_mass = 1.000 kg`, **`balance_step = 0.100 kg`** — ten times coarser than
every other nominal case.

Seven components: six at fraction `0.142857`, one at `0.142858`.

Each nominal dose is ≈ 0.1429 kg, that is **1.429 steps**, rounded down to 1 step —
0.100 kg weighed instead of 0.143. Seven components lose ≈ 0.43 step each:

```
sum of rounded = 0.700 kg
residual       = +0.300 kg = exactly 3 steps = P-02
```

Three steps **equals** the bound, so `RG-040` accepts. The residual goes to `CMP-G`, the
largest fraction, which receives **0.400 kg**. **Final sum = 1.000 kg.**

## Why the case exists

For two things at once, and both are business decisions.

**The bound is strict.** `RG-040` rejects when the residual is `> P-02` steps, not `≥`. A
residual of exactly three steps is acceptable. Written the other way, this batch would be
refused — a batch the formulation manager considers fine. This is the only case in the set
that sits on the bound, so an implementation with `≥` fails **here and nowhere else**.

**The step is not 0.001 kg.** Every other nominal case uses a millimetre-of-a-kilogram
step, where "round the number of steps" and "round to three decimals" happen to agree. At
`0.100 kg` they part company: rounding 0.1429 to three decimals gives 0.143, which is not
a multiple of the step and breaks `INV-02` on the spot. `RG-020` says the rounding applies
to a **number of steps**, and this is the case that makes the difference visible.

> A residual of 3 steps on a 10-step batch is 30 % of the mass moved onto one component.
> That is what [`DEVIATIONS.md`](../DEVIATIONS.md) investigates: the code is conforming, and
> the specification is the thing at fault.

## What it does not test

| | |
|---|---|
| The rejection above the bound | Accepted here. That is [CT-09](CT-09.md), built for it |
| The tie-break | `CMP-G` has the strictly largest fraction — by one part in a million, on purpose |
| The rounding mode | 1.429 steps is not a tie: `HALF_EVEN` and `HALF_UP` agree. That is [CT-10](CT-10.md)'s job |
