## The data

Eight readings: five flagged `FAULTY`, one `ABSENT`, two usable.

| Time | Temperature | Flag | Kept by `RG-010`? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00 | 2.0 | VALID | yes |
| 01:00 | 1.5 | VALID | yes |
| 02:00 | 0.5 | FAULTY | no — flagged |
| 03:00 | −0.5 | FAULTY | no — flagged |
| 04:00 | −1.0 | FAULTY | no — flagged |
| 05:00 | 0.0 | FAULTY | no — flagged |
| 06:00 | 1.0 | FAULTY | no — flagged |
| 07:00 | **ABSENT** | VALID | no — missing |

Two usable, `P-03` = 6. **Expected: rejected with `E-WTH-003`, before any statistic is
computed.**

## Why the case exists

Because a mean over two readings is still a number, and a number gets published.

Nothing about the degraded input is loud. Two readings give a mean, a minimum, a maximum, a
standard deviation and a quality label — all well-formed, all defensible-looking on a
dashboard, none of them a summary of a day. `RG-020` is where the business says how much
data it is prepared to stand behind, and `P-03` = 6 is that number.

The case also exercises **two of the three grounds for discarding a reading**: the quality
flag and the ABSENT value. Both matter:

- **`ABSENT` is not `−999`.** A missing reading encoded as an impossible number survives
  every range check ever written and lands in the mean. The specification bans the
  convention outright, and this is the case where a missing value actually occurs.
- **`FAULTY` readings are discarded, not down-weighted.** The acquisition chain knows
  something the calculation does not.

> **The rejection happens before the statistics, and that ordering is visible in the
> report.** An implementation that computes first and checks afterwards would produce the
> same error code here — and would have logged, cached or emitted a partial summary on the
> way. `RG-020` is placed where it is for that reason.

## What it does not test

| | |
|---|---|
| The third ground of `RG-010` | No reading falls outside `−90 .. +60`. That branch of the filter is exercised by no case |
| `SUSPECT` readings | Only `FAULTY` appears here. `RG-010` discards both, and only one is tested |
| The boundary of `P-03` | Two usable against a threshold of six. **No case sits at exactly 6 usable readings**, where one reading fewer would flip the outcome — that is the boundary, and it is untested |
| Any nominal behaviour | The calculation is never entered |
