| Leg | Distance | Speed | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 km | 5.0 km/h | 6.000000 h |
| 2 | 44 km | 22.0 km/h | 2.000000 h |

`74 km ÷ 8 h` = **exactly 9.25** → `HALF_EVEN` publishes **9.2**; `HALF_UP` would publish 9.3.

**What it catches.** The rounding mode. Before this case, `P-01` was chosen by the fleet
manager, written into the parameter table with an effective date, implemented, and reported
as covered — while **no case produced a quotient a change of mode could move**. Swap
`HALF_EVEN` for `HALF_UP` and the whole suite stayed green.

**Both leg durations are exact — 6 h and 2 h — and that is deliberate.** A non-terminating
duration would nudge the quotient a hair off 9.25, both modes would agree again, and the
case would quietly become [CT-02](CT-02.md).

**What it lets through.** A tie that rounds **up**: 9.25 goes down to the even neighbour, and
no case covers the mirror.
