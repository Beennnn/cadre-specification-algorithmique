## The data

| Leg | Distance | Speed | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 km | 7.0 km/h | 10 ⁄ 7 = 1.428571… h |
| 2 | 10 km | 13.0 km/h | 10 ⁄ 13 = 0.769230… h |

```
total_duration = 10⁄7 + 10⁄13 = 10 × 20⁄91 = 200⁄91 h
total_distance = 20 km
average_speed  = 20 ÷ (200⁄91) = 20 × 91⁄200 = 9.1 km/h        exactly
```

**Expected: `average_speed` = 9.1 km/h.**

## Why the case exists

Neither leg duration has a finite decimal form. **The quotient does** — exactly 9.1 — and
the gap between those two facts is the whole case.

An implementation that carries its intermediates in a fixed, short decimal scale loses the
tail of both durations, and the loss does not cancel. Round each duration to three decimals
and the total becomes 2.198 instead of 200⁄91 = 2.197802…; the published speed comes out at
9.0992…, which rounds to 9.1 and hides the error. Round to one decimal and the total is 2.2,
the speed 9.0909…, still 9.1. **This case does not fail on those** — decimal loss at this
scale is too small to cross a tenth.

What it does establish is that the correct answer is a *clean* number reached through
*unclean* intermediates. If an implementation publishes 9.0 or 9.2 here, the arithmetic is
not slightly imprecise, it is structurally wrong — most likely the arithmetic mean, which
gives `(7.0 + 13.0) ÷ 2 = 10.0 km/h`.

> **The case was renamed in v1.1.0.** It used to be called *Rounding*, and it tested no
> rounding at all. The title claimed a coverage the data did not deliver, and it took
> writing this document to notice. The rounding mode is now tested by
> [CT-06](CT-06.md), which was added for that reason.

## What it does not test

| | |
|---|---|
| [`P-01`](../spec/SPEC-SPD-001.en.md#p-01) | The exact quotient is 9.1: `HALF_EVEN` and `HALF_UP` agree, so the parameter is never arbitrated |
| [`P-02`](../spec/SPEC-SPD-001.en.md#p-02) | 9.1 at one decimal and 9.10 at two are the same number |
| Precision loss in general | The tolerance here is a tenth of a km/h, and decimal truncation at any reasonable scale stays well under it. A case that genuinely fails on short precision would need a quotient sitting near a tie — which is [CT-06](CT-06.md) |
