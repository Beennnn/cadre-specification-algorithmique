| Leg | Distance | Speed | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 km | 7.0 km/h | 10 ⁄ 7 = 1.428571… h |
| 2 | 10 km | 13.0 km/h | 10 ⁄ 13 = 0.769230… h |

`20 km ÷ (200⁄91) h` → **9.1 km/h**, exactly.

**What it catches.** Neither leg duration has a finite decimal form; the quotient does. The
case says the right answer is a clean number reached through unclean intermediates — publish
9.0 or 9.2 here and the arithmetic is structurally wrong, most likely the naive mean, which
gives 10.0.

**What it lets through.** The rounding mode — 9.1 is exact, so `HALF_EVEN` and `HALF_UP`
agree.

> **The case was renamed in v1.1.0.** It used to be called *Rounding*, and it tested no
> rounding at all. Writing this page is what showed it; [CT-06](CT-06.md) was added for the
> job the title claimed.
