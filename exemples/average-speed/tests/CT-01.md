| Leg | Distance | Speed | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 60 km | 30.0 km/h | 2.000000 h |
| 2 | 60 km | 60.0 km/h | 1.000000 h |

`120 km ÷ 3 h` → **40.0 km/h**

**What it catches.** The average of the leg speeds — `(30 + 60) ÷ 2 = 45.0 km/h`. That is
the natural reading of "average speed", it is wrong, and **this is the only case in the set
that rejects it**. The distances are equal so the two readings pull as far apart as they
can: the slow leg takes twice as long, so it weighs twice as much.

**What it lets through.** The rounding mode — the quotient is exactly 40 ([CT-06](CT-06.md)).
The rejection path ([CT-05](CT-05.md)).
