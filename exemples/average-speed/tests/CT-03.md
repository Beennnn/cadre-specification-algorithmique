100 km at 80.0 km/h → 1.25 h → **80.0 km/h**

**What it catches.** The lower bound of the input type: `Sequence[Leg](1 .. 200)` — one leg
is a legal journey. It kills an accumulator initialised with the first element and looped
from index 1, an average divided by `size − 1`, a `reduce` with no identity value.

**What it lets through.** Any weighting error: one leg cannot be mis-weighted, so the naive
mean gives 80.0 as well. The upper bound of 200 legs, which no case exercises.
