| Leg | Distance | Speed | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 km | 30.0 km/h | 1.000000 h |
| 2 | 60 km | 60.0 km/h | 1.000000 h |

`90 km ÷ 2 h` → **45.0 km/h** — and the average of the speeds gives 45.0 too.

**What it catches: nothing, and that is why it is here.** The two methods coincide exactly
when the legs take the same time. Kept in the set, this case makes visible that *a green
suite is not a proof*: a wrong implementation passes it. The
[test report](../reports/TEST-REPORT.md) says so on its own line — *does not discriminate*.

**What it lets through.** Everything [CT-01](CT-01.md) catches. The two cases are the same
shape with one distance changed; that single difference decides whether a case is worth
anything as a detector.
